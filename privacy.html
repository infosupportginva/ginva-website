Privacy Policy

GINVA — Your Life OS

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

GINVA is a personal life management app — calendar, people, fitness and budget.

Contact: infosupport@ginva.app

GINVA is the data controller for the personal data processed through the app.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data — email address, display name, and optional profile photo, when you create an account.
  • Calendar data — events, tasks, meeting links, locations, notes, and attendee names/emails that you enter.
  • Fitness data — workouts, meals, calories, and body weight that you log. This is health-related data; it is stored only to show you your own history and statistics and is never shared or sold.
  • Budget data — budgets, expenses, and bill reminders that you enter. We never connect to your bank and never see real financial accounts.
  • Subscription status — managed by RevenueCat and the Apple App Store / Google Play. We never see your payment card details.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, location tracking data, or contacts beyond attendees you explicitly add.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored securely with Supabase (hosted on AWS in the European Union). All connections are encrypted (TLS). Access is protected by row-level security — only your authenticated account can read your data.

4. AI features

The App includes optional AI assistants (Budget Coach, Fitness Assistant, Weekly Summary). When you use them, relevant excerpts of your data (e.g. recent expenses or workouts) are sent to Groq, Inc. (USA) to generate a response. This data is processed transiently to answer your question and is not used to train AI models. If you never open the AI features, no data is sent.

5. Emails

When you invite someone to an event who does not have a GINVA account, we send them an invitation email (with a calendar attachment) via Resend, Inc. Their email address is used solely to deliver that invitation.

6. Third-party processors

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage (EU)
  • Groq — AI responses (USA, transient processing)
  • Resend — transactional invitation emails
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Apple / Google — payment processing, push notifications
  • Zoom / Jitsi — only if you choose to generate a meeting link

We do not sell your personal data to anyone, and we do not show ads.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, you have the right to access, correct, export, and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Delete your account: Profile → Delete Account inside the App permanently erases your account and all data. You can also email us at infosupport@ginva.app — we respond within 30 days.

8. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

We do not sell or share your personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA. You have the right to know, delete, and correct your personal information — contact us at the email above.

9. Other regions

We process personal information in accordance with Japan's APPI, Korea's PIPA, and Australia's Privacy Act. For requests under these laws, contact infosupport@ginva.app.

10. Data retention

Your data is kept while your account exists. Deleting your account removes all your data from our systems immediately; encrypted backups expire within 30 days.

11. Children

GINVA is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as the App evolves. Material changes will be announced in the App. The current version is always available at this page.
